## ⚠️ CRITICAL CORRECTION: Admission Process

**The current site incorrectly states KEAM rank or LBC entrance exam is required.**

**Actual admission process:**
- BPT admissions in Kerala are based on **12th marks (PCB) ONLY** — no entrance exam required
- Admissions are handled via **LBS Centre for Science & Technology (lbscentre.in)** through their **Centralized Allotment Process (CAP)**
- Eligibility: Pass in 12th with Physics, Chemistry, Biology — minimum 50% aggregate in PCB (45% for SC/ST)
- Students register on LBS portal, submit 12th marks, and seats are allotted based on merit rank
- **KEAM is NOT required for BPT** — KEAM is for Engineering/Pharmacy only
- There is no separate "LBC entrance exam" for BPT either
- Update all admission-related text on the site to reflect: *"Admission via LBS CAP based on 12th PCB marks"*

## GENERAL INFORMATION TO ADD TO THE SITE

### About BPT in Kerala
- **Duration:** 4.5 years (4 years academic + 6 months compulsory internship)
- **Degree Awarded:** Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT) by Kerala University of Health Sciences (KUHS), Thrissur
- **Regulatory Bodies:** KUHS + Kerala Para-Medical Council (KPMC) for registration
- **After BPT:** Can practice independently, pursue MPT (PG), work in hospitals/clinics/sports teams/armed forces/abroad

### Admission Process — CORRECTED
- **Route:** LBS Centre for Science & Technology Centralized Allotment Process (CAP)
- **Eligibility:** 12th pass with Physics, Chemistry, Biology — minimum 50% in PCB aggregate (45% for SC/ST)
- **No entrance exam needed** — merit based on 12th PCB percentage alone
- **Apply at:** lbscentre.in
- **Process:** Register → Upload 12th marks → Rank assigned → Options entered → Allotment → Admission
- **Seat types:** Government seats, Merit seats (LBS-allotted in self-financing colleges), NRI seats, Management quota seats
- **Important:** KEAM is NOT required. There is no BPT entrance exam in Kerala.

### Scholarships Available
- DC scholarship (for Kerala domicile students in private colleges)
- SC/ST scholarships — government colleges nearly free
- OBC post-matric scholarship
- Minority scholarships (for Muslim/Christian minority institutions)
