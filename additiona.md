## ADMISSION PROCESS — CORRECTED TEXT FOR SITE

> **How to apply for BPT in Kerala:**
> BPT admissions are conducted by the **LBS Centre for Science & Technology** through the **Centralized Allotment Process (CAP)**. There is **no entrance exam** — admission is based entirely on your **12th standard PCB marks**.
>
> **Eligibility:** Pass in 12th with Physics, Chemistry, Biology. Minimum 50% aggregate in PCB (45% for SC/ST candidates).
>
> **Steps:** Register at lbscentre.in → Submit 12th mark details → Receive rank → Fill college options → Get allotment → Report for admission.
>
> **Note:** KEAM is not required for BPT. There is no separate BPT entrance exam in Kerala.
